# Updatium Code of Conduct

- Treat every human with respect: be kind and welcoming, no harassment allowed.
- Always assume good faith: always ask the person you communicate with for clarification before jumping to conclusions
  to avoid misunderstandings.
- Respect everyone's time and resources: Remember that people contribute in their free time. Be patient and appreciative of
  their efforts.

## Language policy

English is the primary language used for Updatium's development and communication. However, if you are not confident enough
with your English skills, that's completely fine. You are free to use tools to bypass the language barrier, just make sure
to follow these simple rules:

- You are **allowed** to write in your native language.
- You are also **allowed** to use Google translate to write titles, descriptions and comments.
- You are **allowed** to use LLMs for writing titles and/or issue descriptions.
- However, please **do not** use AI for replying to comments related to bugs or new features. AI is good for translation and
  writing, but when it comes to technical details, I need to discuss them with the *human* to properly understand what needs to
  be improved, without the *AI* in between.

## Enforcing the Code of Conduct

If someone violates these rules, appropriate action will be taken based on the nature of the interaction:
* **In interactions with me (@omeritzics, the maintainer):** Severe or disrespectful behavior will result in being ignored or
  permanently banned, depending on the severity.
  
* **In interactions with other community members:** A warning will be given for the first offense. Repeated violations will lead
  to a permanent ban.

Hopefully, no one will be banned, as I believe our community has good intentions. I have no intention to ban users forever, but
my time is limited, and I prefer to invest it in improving Updatium. If you encounter an unpleasant experience communicating
with someone else (including myself), please ping me to let me know.
